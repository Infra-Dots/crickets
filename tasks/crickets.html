Crickets

Label Deployments/StatefulSets whose Ingress or LoadBalancer saw no traffic in Prometheus over a window. It labels and never deletes. The simulator below runs the e2e fixture cluster from the plan.

Today

  • Idle apps are found by someone eyeballing Grafana, or not at all.
  • kor finds unreferenced resources, not unused ones. An Ingress that still routes counts as "used".
  • Kubecost uses pod network bytes, and health checks and scrapes count as traffic.
  • Cloud LB metrics sit in CloudWatch/Stackdriver/Azure Monitor, and nothing joins them back to a workload.

With Crickets

  • workload → Service → Ingress / LB → provider profile → PromQL.
  • Health is checked at now and at now − window, so a dead exporter or short retention gives unknown, not idle.
  • Idle only when every exposure is known and ≤ threshold.
  • Server-side apply of a label plus an evidence annotation. No rollout, and the label is removed when traffic returns.
